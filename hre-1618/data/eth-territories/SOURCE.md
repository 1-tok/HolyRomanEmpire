# ETH Zurich: territories of the Holy Roman Empire (manual set)

- Creator: Ramona Roller, ETH Zurich, Chair of Systems Design (Schweitzer group)
- Dataset: "Spatio-temporal data on territories of the Holy Roman Empire", 2021-03-02
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000472583
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
- Basis: manual vectorisation of the maps in Schindling & Walter (1989), Die Territorien des Reichs im Zeitalter der Reformation und Konfessionalisierung, and Wikipedia pages on HRE territories.
- Files: territories_manual.* (territory polygons); polygons_manual.* (companion set, same licence).
