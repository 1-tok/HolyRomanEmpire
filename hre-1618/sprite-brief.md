# Holy Roman Empire, AD 1618: sprite brief

Each sprite is a small hand-drawn vignette of the place as it stood in 1618, in the same hand as the Ottonian sheet. Draw the building that stood then, and leave out what came later. Where a building was unfinished in 1618, draw it unfinished.

Common rules
- Transparent PNG, square canvas, no text, no ground shadow, no paper halo.
- Ink outline with a soft wobble, tonal fill, lighter west or north-west face, shaded east or south-east face.
- One sprite per place, centred on the spot. The largest cities get the most detail.

## Imperial and royal cities
- Vienna: Stephansdom with its single steep south spire and an unfinished north tower; medieval walls with the Roter Turm; the Hofburg as a Renaissance block. Leave out the Baroque domes.
- Prague: Hradčany castle with St Vitus cathedral, the western towers still unfinished; Charles Bridge with its Gothic towers, no Baroque statues; the Old Town Bridge Tower.
- Regensburg: Dom St Peter with its unfinished twin towers; the old Stone Bridge over the Danube (built 1135 to 1146); Roman walls.
- Augsburg: town hall under construction (1615 to 1620), its tower still rising; the Fuggerei housing.
- Nuremberg: Kaiserburg castle on its sandstone hill; twin-towered St Sebald and St Lorenz churches; the Schöner Brunnen fountain.
- Frankfurt: the Römer town hall with its stepped gables; the Kaiserdom St Bartholomäus; the stone Main bridge.
- Cologne: the Dom as an unfinished Gothic building, with its stump of a tower and a crane on top; Great St Martin; Roman walls with gates; the Rhine port.
- Magdeburg: Gothic cathedral with twin towers (destroyed 1631, so draw it intact); the Elbe.
- Lübeck: Marienkirche in brick Gothic with twin towers; the Holstentor gate; the town hall.
- Milan: Duomo with its facade still unfinished and scaffolded, many spires; Castello Sforzesco; walled city.

## Archbishoprics, bishoprics and free cities
- Mainz: cathedral with its Romanesque towers; Electoral palace; the Rhine.
- Strasbourg: Münster with its completed Gothic spire; the Rhine and the island.
- Salzburg: Hohensalzburg fortress on the rock; the cathedral rebuilt from 1614 in the Italian style (under construction in 1618).
- Ulm: the Minster with its tower only, the spire not yet built (completed in 1890). Draw the tower without a spire.
- Trent (Trento): cathedral and the Buonconsiglio castle.
- Breslau (Wrocław): cathedral and the Oder bridges; Silesian Piast-era churches.
- Graz: Schlossberg castle, the Landhaus arcades, the clock tower.
- Innsbruck: Goldenes Dachl with its gilded roof, the Hofkirche.

## Netherlands and north
- Brussels: Gothic Hôtel de Ville with its spire, the Grand Place guildhalls (Gothic and Renaissance, before the 1695 bombardment).
- Antwerp: Cathedral of Our Lady, its tall Gothic spire.
- Amsterdam: Oude Kirk and the Nieuwe Kerk, with their Gothic towers; the harbour and canals.
- Hamburg: harbour and church towers (no Renaissance-to-modern towers); Elbe.
- Lübeck (above): see its own entry.

## Electoral and princely capitals
- Dresden: the Residenzschloss, Renaissance; Elbe bridge.
- Leipzig: the Old Town Hall, Renaissance (1556); St Thomas, Gothic.
- Berlin and Cölln: Berlin Cathedral (Renaissance), the Stadtschloss, the Spree.
- Munich: the Frauenkirche with its two domes (completed 1488; domes from 1525); the Residenz; the Isartor gate.
- Stuttgart: the Stiftskirche, the castle, the Neckar.
- Heidelberg: the castle on the hill, ruined after 1693 but intact in 1618, and the Renaissance Ottheinrich wing.
- Kassel: Landgraves' castle and the city walls.

## Landmarks to show
- Rhine and Danube crossings: the Stone Bridge at Regensburg and the Rhine bridge at Cologne.
- Mining: Goslar Rammelsberg (sign only).
- Salt: Halle and Reichenhall (sign only).
- Battlefields from the period: White Mountain (1620) falls after the 1618 frame, so leave it off.

## Banned in every sprite
- Baroque domes and towers, post-1618 spires, modern roads, railways and fences.
- Any building that was not yet built in 1618, unless the sprite shows it under construction.
